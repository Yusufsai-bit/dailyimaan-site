Support

Daily Imaan is a free Qur'an companion: a verse a day, prayer times you can tune to your local mosque, Qibla, adhkar, tasbih, and gentle daily reminders. No ads, no accounts, every feature is free.

Contact. Questions, feedback, or something not working? Email us at support@dailyimaan.com and we'll get back to you.

Prayer times don't match my mosque? Open Settings and try a different calculation method, switch between Standard and Hanafi Asr, or nudge any prayer up to 15 minutes earlier or later to match your local mosque.

Is my data private? There is no account, so nothing in the app is tied to your name. Details are in the full Privacy Policy.
