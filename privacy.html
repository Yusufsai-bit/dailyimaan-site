Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026
Daily Imaan is a prayer companion. It works almost entirely on your iPhone. This page explains, in plain language, the few things the app sends off your device and what stays on it.

What stays on your phone. Your bookmarks, reading progress, streak, adhkar counts, and settings are stored on your device. They are not uploaded.

What leaves your phone, and why. Prayer times: the app sends your location (latitude and longitude) to the AlAdhan prayer-times service (aladhan.com), which replies with today's times for where you are. The app has no account, so it sends no name, email, or user ID. Its terms say it keeps request logs for up to 29 days. Recitation audio: when you press Listen, audio streams from the alquran.cloud audio service (or, for Saad Al-Ghamdi's recitation, from the EveryAyah service at everyayah.com), and the same applies to that request. Tafsir: when you open an ayah's tafsir, the text is fetched from Quran.com's public API and kept on your phone so you can read it again without a connection. The app attaches no name, email, or account identifier to these requests; as with any internet service, the providers receive the technical network address and ordinary request data needed to answer. Everything else (the Quran text, hadith, adhkar, the 99 names, and duas) is bundled inside the app and works fully offline.

What the app does not do. No accounts or sign-ups. No advertising. Your information is never sold or shared with advertisers. The app sends no analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for. Location, to calculate prayer times for where you are and to point the qibla compass. If you decline, location-based features will be limited and everything else keeps working. Notifications, optional, for the daily ayah and prayer reminders you choose to turn on. Both can be changed in iOS Settings at any time.

Children. The app asks no one, of any age, for a name, email, or other personal details. Its use of location is described above.

Changes. If what the app sends or stores ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
